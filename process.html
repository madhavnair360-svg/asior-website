Step By Step

From Logo To Delivered Order.

Five steps, start to finish. Click any step below to see exactly what we need from you and what you get back.

01

Tell Us What You Need

Send your logo or idea and roughly how many pieces. No obligation, no guesswork.

What We Need From You

  • Your logo or design idea
  • Roughly how many pieces
  • Garment type you're thinking

What You Get Back

  • A real reply, not an auto-ticket
  • An honest read on whether it's a fit
02

Free Design & Quote

We put together real pricing and a mockup on the actual garment — usually within 24 hours.

What We Need From You

  • Any size or color preferences
  • A budget range, if you have one

What You Get Back

  • Real pricing, not a placeholder
  • A mockup on the actual garment
  • A reply within 24 hours
03

Free Sample

A proto sample is cut and sewn for your approval. Changes happen here, before bulk production starts.

What We Need From You

  • Sign-off on the mockup
  • A shipping address

What You Get Back

  • A physical sample to feel and inspect
  • Room to request changes before bulk
04

Production

Once the sample's approved, we schedule the full run. 3–4 weeks, on schedule.

What We Need From You

  • Final approval on the sample
  • Confirmed quantity

What You Get Back

  • 3–4 weeks to a finished run
  • A heads-up if anything changes
05

Delivery & Reorders

Every piece is checked by hand before it ships. Reordering the same fit later is just as easy.

What We Need From You

  • Nothing — reorders reuse your saved spec

What You Get Back

  • Every piece checked by hand
  • The same fit and finish, next time

What To Send Us

You don't need everything figured out — this just helps us move faster.

  • ✓

    Your Logo or Idea

    A vector file if you have one (.ai, .eps, .pdf, .svg). If not, a PNG works fine — we'll clean it up as part of the free design help.

  • ✓

    Rough Quantity

    Even a ballpark helps us quote accurately. 1 piece or 1,000 — there's no wrong answer.

  • ✓

    Garment Preference

    Tee, hoodie, polo, shorts — or just tell us the vibe and we'll suggest blanks that fit.

  • ✓

    Your Timeline

    Working toward an event or a date? Say so up front and we'll tell you straight whether we can hit it.

Ready for step one?

Send us your idea, sketch, or reference images and we'll scope the project with you.
